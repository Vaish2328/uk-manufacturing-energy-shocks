The data files are derived from public UK official-statistics sources, primarily the Office for National Statistics (ONS) and the Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ). Source data remain subject to the providers' applicable terms, including the Open Government Licence where applicable.

This repository does not relicense third-party data. Users should cite and verify the original publications listed in `docs/source_manifest.csv`.
